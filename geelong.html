Your Geelong froyo stop

🍦 What's in the machines

Eight signature flavours churned fresh every morning, including açai and a plant-based strawberry. See today's lineup.

🌾 Gluten free first

All of our yogurts are gluten free except Biscoff, Cookies & Cream and Tiramisu. Full allergen info for every topping too.

⚖️ One price, everything in

Build your bowl, load the topping bar, and pay $4.19 per 100g. Yogurt and toppings included.
